What it does

One app for everything you fetch.

Links, torrents and videos used to need three programs. Blister handles all of them, and the boring parts too.

BLS-01HTTP

Up to 64 lanes per file

Sixteen connections by default, up to sixty-four. When one lane finishes early it takes half of the slowest lane's work, so nothing sits idle at the end.

BLS-02TORRENT

Torrents without the clutter

Magnets and .torrent files, with DHT, UPnP and peer encryption. Pick the files you want, watch the pieces land, and stop seeding at the ratio you set.

BLS-03VIDEO
YouTubeXInstagramTikTokRedditVimeoTwitch.m3u8

Video from the sites you use

Paste a page link and Blister hands it to yt-dlp, picks the best format and merges audio and video with ffmpeg. Raw HLS streams work too.

BLS-04RESUME
  1. 61% power cut
  2. ··· reboot
  3. 61% picks up here

Survives a crash or a reboot

Progress is saved as it goes and disk space is reserved up front, so a crash, dropped Wi-Fi or a restart costs you seconds, not the whole file.

BLS-05QUEUE
01:00→07:00 3 at a time

A queue that behaves

Set how many downloads run at once and the rest wait their turn. Give it a window, say one in the morning to seven, and it only works while you sleep.

BLS-06LIMITS
8 MB/s 1 MB/s

Speed limits that hold

Cap everything, cap one download, or cap uploads. Your video call and your game keep their share of the line.

BLS-07VERIFY
sha256 9f86d081…b0f00a08 ✓ match

Checks what it downloaded

Paste the checksum from the download page and Blister compares it when the file lands. MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256 and SHA-512.

BLS-08SORT
Videos/Music/Archives/Programs/Documents/

Files land where they belong

Every download gets a sensible name and goes to the right folder by type. No more "download (7).zip" in one giant pile.

BLS-09CATCH
browser → 127.0.0.1:7323 clipboard → caught this page → 24 links

Catches links for you

The browser extension hands downloads over a local, token-guarded port. Copy a link anywhere and it's caught, or grab every link on a page at once.
